Privacy Policy

This policy explains how "Work Log" handles data.

1. Data stored

  • Your name, work and off-day records, work places, and recorded amounts.
  • This data is stored on your device only, and is not sent to any server operated by the developer.

2. Location

  • Location is used only to detect the nearest work place when adding a new day.
  • Location is not stored or shared.
  • Coordinates are sent to the weather service Open-Meteo to show weather conditions. That service has its own privacy policy.

3. Advertising

  • The app may show ads from Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising ID on your device. You can limit ad personalization in your device settings.

4. Notifications

  • The daily reminder runs locally on your device.

5. Selling and sharing

  • The developer does not sell or rent your data to third parties.

6. Your choices

  • You can back up your data, clear a month from Settings, or clear browser data to remove it from your device.

7. Children

  • The app is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes

  • This policy may be updated, and the updated version will appear on this page.
